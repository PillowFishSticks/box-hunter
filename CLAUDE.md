# Box Hunter: notes for Claude

The owner (PillowFishSticks) isn't a developer and wants Claude to own the code. Keep it simple and cheap to maintain: one HTML file, no frameworks, no build step, no runtime dependencies. **Don't ask before pushing: commit and push when checks pass** (owner's standing instruction). **Bump `VERSION` in index.html on every push** (patch for fixes, minor for features); the owner checks it in the 💾 settings card to see an update landed.

## What it is
A pixel-art incremental game about opening mystery boxes, in the spirit of Scritchy Scratchy (everything is an object on a desk/bench, a satisfying hand action you later automate, prestige) and Cookie Clicker (rising costs, milestones, surprise events). Sister project to Bulk Season (`PillowFishSticks/bulk-season`), whose save, input and sim-bot patterns are reused. Desktop-first (the owner plays fullscreen on a TV, with a mouse); landscape phones second.

The full scope lives in the design doc "Box Hunter: Game Design Doc" (a Claude doc artifact): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/93ef27de-3f42-46c3-98a6-1c8f5869d3c9. The owner asked for everything at once after playtest 1, so all six phases are built (polish ongoing). Original plan: 1 first playable → 2 economy (shelves, scrap/parts, 3 box tiers, sim bot) → 3 automation (kid, van, sorter, conveyor, offline) → 4 prestige ("Sell the business", reputation stars) → 5 content (8 box types, ~150 items, album, legendaries, cursed items, events) → 6 polish and release. **Two owner playtests:** after phase 1 (does opening feel good?) and after phase 4 (is prestige worth it?).

Owner decisions: name Box Hunter; the shop pet is a dog; full game **3–4 hours** (owner, 18 Oct; was 6–8); no real brands (the post sack is "Second Class Post Co."); never real money, ads or paid boxes. Still open: whether prestige gets a story beat (a rival chain buying you out).

## Files
- `index.html`: the whole game (CSS, markup, JS).
- `fonts/`: Silkscreen (headings; "Silk"; canvas labels use `SilkC`, a copy without the digit override), Pixelify Sans (body, buttons, money; "Pix") and Jersey 10, OFL, self-hosted. Pixelify's `liga`/`dlig` lookups are emptied in the font file (its "fi" ligature drew as "A"). **Both pixel fonts have unreadable digits** (owner couldn't tell 4 from 9), so the `@font-face` rules split by `unicode-range`: digits, £, %, . , / + × come from Jersey 10 inside both "Pix" and "Silk". All faces are loaded up front so the canvas has them.
- `README.md`: player-facing.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icon-*.png`: it's a PWA (Add to Home Screen, fullscreen landscape, offline). `sw.js` is network-first, so updates show up on the next load; bump `CACHE` if the file list changes.

## How it's built
- **Stage:** a fixed 480×270 `#stage` scaled to fit (`fit()`). The scene is a 480×270 canvas of `fillRect` pixel art (`R()`); the room is cached in `ROOM` (`buildRoom()`, garage → unit after 1 sale → warehouse after 5). UI panels are DOM over it. Never draw numbers on the canvas at small sizes (owner found them unreadable): counts are DOM tags (`#qTag`, `#hTag`).
- **Goals hand out what they ask for** (owner: no grinding for something the game told you to do): a goal with `give` ({box,n} / {up} / {lic}) makes that exact thing free where you'd normally buy it (owner: the Free goes on the thing you click, not on the goal bar): the box row's price says Free, the licence says 🔓 Free, the upgrade row says Free and All upgrades shows a FREE badge and opens that tab (`gift()`, `isGiftUp/Box/Lic`, `claimGoal()`). The free thing glows (`.gift`); the goal bar says nothing about it. Each freebie can be claimed once per goal (`S.gifted`). Early goals: first parcel (free) → load it → open 5 boxes (5 free parcels) → Sharper Cutter (free) → Fast Hands (free) → bin the heap → rare find → shelve a find → scrap a find. **All freebies come first** (owner): after Fast Hands nothing is ever Free again: from the loader on, goals just point the way and pay a small cash reward. Goals for doing things keep cash `rew`. The sim claims them too. **Alternate menu goals with play goals** (owner: five "hire X" goals in a row meant living in the upgrades menu): after each upgrade goal comes a hands-on one (open 25 boxes, sell 10 finds, bin the heap 5×, find 3 Rares, open 10 envelopes, sell 3 off shelves, scrap 10, open 10 luggage). Goals count in any order (owner): `checkGoals()` checks every goal, marks it in `S.gdone` and pays it the moment it's met, and the goal bar flashes a gold `#goalCel` card (always the goal itself and what it paid: "✅ Goal done!" / "✅ Done early!" + emoji, title, reward; owner: never just "goal done"); `S.goal` is the first goal not yet done. A 1s check catches goals finished by automation. Clicking the goal bar opens `#goalModal` (`openGoals()`): done ✅ with rewards, the current one with progress, the next 4. `S.gdone` and `S.gifted` are keyed by goal **title** (`S.gv` 4), so goals can be reordered; renaming a goal needs an entry in `GOAL_REN`. Old saves are converted in `fixGoalIdx()` (`GOALS_V1`, `GOALS_V3`). The All upgrades button shows a green FREE tag next to its red count while an upgrade is free.
- **Layout:** top: till (cash, ⚙ parts, ⭐ stars), goal bar (`GOALS`, one line each, keep texts short), tools (help, album, sound, music, save, fullscreen). Left: Box shop (`BOXES`, ×1/×5/×10 + Bulk Buy sizes, 🚚 corner picks the van's box, at most 6 rows: the priciest unlocked + the next locked) and the All upgrades button. Right: finds tray (`TRAY` = 9) and "Your business": the 5-step chain Buy → Load → Open → Sell → Bin, each row YOU or AUTO, click to open that tab. Left column bottom: the bonus event banner (`#ev`) and messages (`#toast`); nothing may sit over the bench or shelves (owner). Bench: queue stack (left), mat with the box, empties heap + baler (right), staff row + conveyor + machines (bottom), sorter, dog, shop shelves on the wall, van outside the window.
- **No controller support** (owner removed it: too buggy). Keyboard: Space act, B buy, S sell all, J sell junk, K scrap all, U upgrades, E grab the mystery van, X bin empties, arrows move focus, Esc closes.
- **Keep text light** (owner: "a bit overboard with usability"): no `title` tooltips on buttons, no help cards (`intro()` is switched off), no floating labels, short find pop-up buttons. Guidance comes from the goal bar and the one-line hint. The only hover tip left is on canvas things (shelf items, the heap). Pop-ups close on any click outside them.
- **No browser pop-ups** (owner): never `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt`; use the in-game `ask(title,text,yesLabel,danger,fn)` card (`#askModal`, Cancel focused).
- **Modals:** fit the screen (`.modal>.panel` is a flex column, only `.scroll` scrolls), ✕ top-right, click the backdrop or Esc to close. No bottom close buttons.
- **Upgrades screen** (`#upModal`, owner: everything in one place, nothing opened by clicking bench objects): tabs follow the business chain (owner: the old Hands/Shop/Machines tabs were confusing): 1 Buy (van, bulk, haggling, accountant) / 2 Load (loader, X-ray) / 3 Open (cutter, Fast Hands, box cutter, luck) / Bench (staff, training, conveyor, machines) / 4 Sell (sorter, shelves, sign, till) / 5 Bin (baler, heap) / Stars (`TABS`, `u.g`, row order `ORDER`); the Your business rows open the matching tab. **Every upgrade helps the automated business** (owner: hand-only upgrades felt pointless): Sharp Eye is luck for everyone, Fast Hands speeds the whole mat, Lucky Streak counts the box cutter, X-ray level 2 makes the loader take the priciest queued box first. Left: one-line rows (click = buy); right: a details pane (`#upDet`, `renderDet`) for the hovered row with what it does, now/next, cost and a Buy button. No floating tooltips inside it (owner: they hid the list). The sorter rules (`#rulesT`) have their own card, `#rulesModal` (`openRules()`/`renderRules()`), opened by the 🧹 Rules button in the Your Finds header (shown once the sorter is hired) or a button in the Sell tab's details pane (owner: the table was cut off in the details pane). There is no Quick buy (owner removed it); `quickPicks()` is still used by the Accountant and the sim.
- **Core vs visuals:** the economy is a headless core (`coreTick`, `rollBox`, `gain`, `autoOpen`, `buyBoxes`, `sortOne`, `shelfTick`, `eventsTick`, `sellBusiness`) shared by the game and the sim bot; visuals go through the `FX` hooks (no-ops when `SIM`). The core runs on real time; big gaps are caught up in 1s steps (`catchUp`): full rate while the tab is hidden, no offline progress (owner).
- **Boxes:** parcel £5, envelope £40, sack £300, luggage £2.5k, crate £20k, storage £200k (after 1 sale, licence £50M), haunted £2M (2 sales), pallet £10M (3 sales; 12–36 boxes inside), Mystery Vault £100B (4 sales, hand only, ends with credits). Bigger warehouse after 2 sales. Each opens differently (`kind`, `cutLine()`, a `draw*` per kind). Unlocking needs opened counts of the previous box, sometimes sales, and a cash licence (`lic`, bought by clicking the row). Item values are calibrated per box so the average is `ev` × price at base luck (`RM`, `LUCKX`). **Box contents** (owner: not a fixed count): `cnt` things always, each `bonus[]` entry is a chance of one extra (marked `bonus`, a gold square in the reveal, "(N bonus!)" in the caption), `min` makes the first thing at least that rarity: parcel 1; envelope 1 +50%/+20%; sack 5 + five chances; luggage 2 (+40%), one Common+; crate 1 (+35%), one Rare+; storage 12 + eight 50% chances, one Rare+; haunted 2 (+30%), one Epic+. Calibration (`avgCnt`, `E(min)`) keeps each box's value the same. **The reveal shows every find** (best one big, the rest in one or two rows under it), by hand or automatic.
- **Never broke:** a free ♻️ Old cardboard row is always at the top of the shop (`SCRAP`, not in `BOXES`). It drops a sheet on the mat; cutting it pays `SCRAP_PAY` 50p, no item, no empty. No free postie parcels and no buying by clicking the mat (owner found both weird); boxes are only bought in the shop.
- **Shop dog** (`dog`, `drawDog`): wanders along the front of the bench (`DOGY`, `DOGX`), drawn after the box and staff so it's never hidden (owner); stays to the sides while a box is on the mat, trots over to sniff new boxes, hops at rare finds, barks at epics, naps when idle.
- **No album** (owner, 24 Oct: "really dumb"): `needsAlbum()` returns false and `setBonus()` returns 0, so finds always go to the tray and set bonuses are off; the 📖 button is hidden and the album goals are gone. The `SETS`/album code is still there, unused.
- **No key letters on buttons** (owner found them ugly): `.k` is hidden everywhere; the keys still work. The Old cardboard row says Free. Upgrade tabs have no step numbers.
- **Money climbs** (owner: the van emptied the till to £0): the van spends at most `VAN_SHARE` 50% of cash per run. The "▲ £X/min" line was removed (owner: pointless); `#rateV` is hidden.
- **No offline earnings** (owner: "there's no auto money generation"): closing the game pauses it; the "While you were away" receipt is off. A hidden tab still runs at full speed.
- **Finds:** tray items `{i,m}` (m: ×1.2 by hand, ×2 special). Click → pop-up: sell / shelve / album / scrap for parts. Shelves don't earn by sitting there (owner: "how does that make sense?"): a customer buys each item at the shop price, `shelfPay` = `SHELF_K` 2 × the trade price (×2 cursed, ×2 Lucky Cat), at `custRate()` (1%/s, about 100s per item; Shop Sign = more customers). Owner: shelving must clearly beat selling, or automating it isn't worth it. Customers are visible (`custs`, `custTick`, `drawCusts`): they walk along the shop wall, take the reserved item (`e.rsv`), pay and leave with it over their head; some just browse. In the sim/offline they buy instantly. 19 album sets with bonuses (`SETS`), 5 relics (rule changers, kept forever), cursed haunted items with shelf effects (`CURSED`).
- **Hiring lives in Your business** (owner, 24 Oct: buying a one-off loader inside All upgrades was silly): `HIRES` (van, loader, autocut, sorter, baler) are bought from each row's gold "Hire £X" tag in `#bizList`; once hired it says **Upgrade** (opens that tab) with a red count of affordable upgrades (`em.upg`, don't use class `up`: that's the upgrade-row style). Tags are a fixed 44px so nothing changes size; shop price buttons are a fixed 28px and never listed in the upgrades screen or its counts; their speed upgrades stay in the tabs, locked until hired.
- **The crew stays hired when you sell the business** (owner: selling dropped income back to £10/min). The ⭐ "Crew keeps its training" (id `hsVan`) also keeps their speed upgrades.
- **Money pays people, parts pay for things** (owner, 24 Oct): cash buys hires, crew speed upgrades (loader/box cutter/van/sorter/baler), staff, training, the accountant and haggling; ⚙ parts buy tools and objects: Sharper Cutter, Sharp Eye, Fast Hands, Lucky Streak, X-ray, Bulk Buy, Extra Shelves, Shop Sign, Sharp Till, Bigger Corner, conveyor and machines.
- **The chain** (owner): 1 Buy = van, 2 Load = `loader` (until hired, click the queue or the mat to load: `loadNext()`), 3 Open = `autocut` (cuts the mat box after `autoDelayL`, no ×1.2 hand bonus) plus staff and machines for queue boxes, 4 Sell = sorter, 5 Bin = baler. Each has a speed upgrade.
- Clicking a shelved item sells it now or takes it down (`openShelfPop`). `INTROS` help cards exist but are off.
- **The crew** (owner: automation must be people on screen, not things that just happen): `CREW` + `crewTick` + `draw*`: the loader (orange) pushes each box from the queue onto the mat (the box starts at the queue once he's hired); the box cutter (green, upgrade id still `autocut`, was a machine) walks to the cut line and holds the knife while it cuts; the sorter walks each shelved find up to its shelf slot (`e.walk` hides it and keeps customers off it until he arrives; other sorts are an arm wave); the baler hand feeds flats into the baler; the van has a driver. Staff sit on the bench row.
- **Sharper Cutter** (owner: it felt useless once cutting was automatic): `cutK()` = +4% value per level on every mat box (`rollBox` with `hand`, so you and the box cutter; bench staff/machines don't use the knife), plus +18% cut speed and a wider blade.
- **Knife tiers:** `drawCutter` changes with Sharper Cutter level (0 yellow, 1 red, 4 steel with a longer blade, 7 purple with sparkle, 10 crystal with a moving shine); the Golden Cutter relic keeps the gold handle.
- **Finds tray:** fixed 13px header that never wraps (owner: it grew when the count appeared); one list sorted priciest first (display only; `S.tray` order is unchanged); `#trayList` scrolls when full; buttons **Shelve all** (`shelveAllTray()`, key J: fills free shelf spaces with the priciest finds; owner wants shelving and customers to be the main loop; Sell junk was removed as confusing) / **Scrap ⚙** (`scrapAllTray()`, key K, the whole tray) / **Sell all** (key S).
- **Menus never resize on hover:** `#upModal` and `#albModal` panels have a fixed height.
- **Van box picker:** every unlocked shop row shows a faded 🚚 (`.vt.off`) you click to make the van buy that box; the current one is solid.
- **Automation:** staff (`staffRate`, ×1.5 at 10/25/40, cap 3+3×conveyor), training tiers, van (auto-buy), sorter (auto-sell by `S.rules`), baler (auto-bin), accountant (auto-upgrade), machines bought with parts (slicer, arm, spinner). Empties: every opened box adds one to the heap; a full heap stops all opening; click it to bin.
- **Prestige:** stars = floor((run earnings ÷ £10M)^0.4 × 1.15^Big name). Every star ever = +1% value. Star upgrades in four groups (Head starts, Boosts, Keep, plus the Unlocks list).
- **Events:** mystery van (click within 8s), car boot (shelves 3× for 60s), customs auction, lucky hour. Banners say "Bonus event: …".
- **Save:** localStorage `boxHunter.v1` (v3 shape, `migrate()` upgrades phase-1 saves), backup on load, read-only if another tab writes, BH1: export/import codes in the 💾 modal.
- `#debug` skips the title and exposes `window.BH` (incl. `BH.sim(hours,{verbose:1})`).

## Balance
`BH.sim(10)` plays the whole game headlessly (hand opens every 3s, saves for the next unlock licence, buys the cheapest affordable upgrade keeping a reserve, sells the business when the star rate drops, at least 3⭐ for the first sales, at most 35 min a run). Target (owner): **3–4 hours for a person ≈ 2–2.5h for the bot**. Latest (v1.8.0: crew kept on sale, money for people / parts for things, early ev parcel 1.7 / envelope 1.55 / sack 1.35 / luggage 1.2, storage licence £50M, haunted £200M, vault £100B): first sale 21–33 min, vault 2–3.7h (avg ~2.5h). The bot is poor with parts and sometimes sells every 10 min; a person should do better. Levers: `sales` needed per unlock, licence `lic` costs, early box `ev`, the vault price.
