How to play

📦Buy boxes in the Box shop (×1, ×5, ×10 at once). They queue by the mat.
🔪Drag the cutter along the tape, or just hold on the box. Boxes you open yourself are worth 20% more.
💰Click a find on the right to sell, shelve, file in the album or scrap for ⚙ parts.

The glow shows how good a find is: grey junk, green common, blue rare, purple epic, gold legendary. Empty boxes go on the heap in the corner: click it to bin them, or it fills up and stops everything.

Hire staff to open boxes, a van to buy them, a sorter to sell and a baler to bin empties. Click things on the bench to upgrade them. When the 🏷️ sign appears you can sell the business for ⭐ stars and start again stronger. The 🎯 goal always says what to do next.
