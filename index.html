How to play

📦Buy a box from the Box shop on the left.
🔪Drag the cutter along the tape (or just hold on the box) to slice it open.
💰Sell what you find on the right, then buy more boxes.

The glow tells you how good a find is: grey junk, green common, blue rare, purple epic, gold legendary. Rarer boxes rattle more before you open them.

Spend money on upgrades. The 🎯 goal at the top always shows what to do next; goals pay rewards and unlock better boxes. Flatten empty boxes, or the old heap, for a few pennies.
